Apollo Com Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Apollo Com does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it makes no network connections.

Microphone

If you turn on the RECORD switch, Apollo Com uses the microphone only while you hold the push-to-talk button. Recordings are processed and stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to share or export one yourself.

Deleting your data

You can delete any recording from the Transmission Log. Deleting the app removes all of its recordings.
