Apollo Com Support

Apollo Com is a push-to-talk tone generator that recreates the famous "beeps" heard on Apollo-era space-to-ground radio.

How to use it

  • Push to talk: hold the big silver button to hear the 2525 Hz intro tone. Release it to hear the 2475 Hz outro tone.
  • STATIC: slide it to ON to hear radio-channel noise for as long as you hold the button.
  • RECORD: slide it to ON to record your voice while you hold the button. The red lamp lights while it's recording. Each recording sounds like Apollo air-to-ground audio and is framed by the tones.
  • LOG: tap the small LOG button to play, share or delete your recordings.

Common questions

I don't hear anything. Turn up the media volume. The tones play even with the ring/silent switch on.

Recording doesn't work. Make sure RECORD is ON and that microphone access is allowed in Settings › Apollo Com.

Can I use the tones on a phone call? No. iOS doesn't let apps add audio to a phone call. Record a transmission and share it instead.

Where are my recordings? In the LOG, and in the Files app under On My iPhone › Apollo Com.
